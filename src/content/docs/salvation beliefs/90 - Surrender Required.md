- Human authority is ultimate authority in the Tribes.

- They often say: "It's better to be wrong together than right alone." In a practical sense, what this means is that members need to follow leadership even if they know leadership is going in the wrong direction, because being "right alone" is not a tolerated option.

- [check the wording to make sure this quote is accurate] "Now, many people say they believe in God, but they do not trust Him to do with them as He pleases. But we do. When we say, “We trust Him,” it means we literally trust the Body of Messiah, as
salvation comes through the Body. And that is eternal life. No one receives eternal life
unless he trusts the Body of Messiah."
:::

## Response

They are conflating Christ and His body... so severely that they end up *replacing* Christ Himself with His body. 

They say that salvation comes through the body.

But the Bible says that salvation comes specifically through the Head (and the Bible makes a major distinction between the Head and the body).

Each part of the body has a different role (1 Corinthians 12). Only the Head has the role of providing salvation.

The Bible never says to trust the Body (which includes people besides the Savior) for salvation, but rather to trust the Savior specifically.

Acts 4:12 - "And there is salvation in no other, for there is not another name under heaven having been given among men by which it is necessary for us to be saved.”

There's a line (it really exists) between surrendering to the Savior and surrendering to other people.

Even if those people say they are the "Body" of the Savior, the Word still provides for *accountability* for the Body's leadership.

And it still holds the Savior up as a higher authority than the Body's leadership.

In fact, saying "Yes" to the Savior may sometimes *require* saying "No" to regular humans.
