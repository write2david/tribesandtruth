## Response

The Tribes are hung up on the Gospel being presented by one who has authority (themselves, because they've given everything up).

"Someone has to be SENT by God, meaning having his authority, proclaiming all the things Christ commanded"

But that's not in Scripture.

In fact, the opposite is: Paul said that he's happy even if people present the Gospel out of spite.
